### Ingredients

* Salmon
* Olive Oil, to Fry In
* Roasted Red Pepper
* Smoked Paprika
* Oregano
* Lemon Pepper
* Garlic Salt
* Onion Salt
* ¼ Cup Brown Sugar

### Instructions

Heat olive oil in a frypan. Season salmon with roasted red pepper, smoked paprika, oregano, lemon pepper, garlic salt, onion salt, and brown sugar. Fry until salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
