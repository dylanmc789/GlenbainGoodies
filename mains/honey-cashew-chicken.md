### Ingredients

* 1 Cup Water
* ¼ Cup Cornstarch
* ¼ Cup Brown Sugar
* ¼ Cup Soy Sauce
* 1–2 Cups Chopped Cashews
* ½ Tsp. Ginger
* ½ Tsp. Garlic
* 1 Cup Honey
* Splash of Lemon Juice

### Instructions

In a pot, mix water and cornstarch until dissolved. Whisk in remaining ingredients. Cook on medium heat, bringing sauce to a boil, or until it thickens.

Season chicken breast with salt and pepper. In a fry pan with oil, brown chicken breasts on both sides. Once chicken is browned, place in a buttered baking dish. Sprinkle cashews over chicken and pour sauce over top.

Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes or until chicken is fully cooked.
